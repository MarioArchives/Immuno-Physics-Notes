Quantitative Immunology for Physicists

Grégoire Altan-Bonnet, Thierry Mora and Aleksandra M. Walczak. Physics Reports 849, 1–83 (2020). arXiv, journal.

How physics enters immunology, from the rate of a single binding event to the co-evolution of viruses and host populations: binding kinetics, ligand discrimination, cytokine signaling, cell fate, receptor repertoires, clone dynamics, affinity maturation and viral evolution.

How to read these notes

Each page follows one section of the paper and explains its arguments, numbers and equations in plain terms; the wording is new and the paper remains the reference. Every part names the page of the PDF it covers, so the two can be read side by side.

Underlined abbreviations such as TCR, pMHC or IL-2 open a short definition when clicked, and the glossary lists all of them. The framed figures are small simulations of the models in the text: they start when they scroll into view, and their sliders change the parameters under discussion.

Contents

    The paper also has a glossary of biological terms and a long methods section on physical kinetics, gene regulation, and population dynamics and genetics. Both are best read in the original.
